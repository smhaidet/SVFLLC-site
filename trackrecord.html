Capital experience

Personal capital. Private equity. Bank financing.

Marcus' project history includes transactions funded with his own capital as well as projects using private investor equity and commercial bank financing. The objective is not simply to raise capital; it is to deploy it into opportunities where research, structure and execution can create durable value.

Shadow Canyon Estates is a key example: Marcus sourced the opportunity, prepared the business plan, raised approximately $1 million of private equity from three investors, secured bank financing through San Luis Bank & Trust, and oversaw the project through mapping, infrastructure development and lot sales.
