Bakersfield, California · Multifamily redevelopment · 2002

Eight-Unit Apartment Redevelopment

Acquired an eight-unit apartment property, completed a targeted capital-improvement program, improved the units and operating income, and sold the repositioned asset.

  • Purchased for approximately $220,000 and owned directly by Marcus Haidet
  • Approximately $80,000 invested in property improvements

  • Interior refreshes completed across the apartment units
  • Rents increased following the physical and operational repositioning
