La Piña Boutique Hotel

An operating boutique hotel owned and controlled by SVF, with an established rental history and a planned five-unit rooftop expansion designed around direct ocean orientation, larger-format guest amenities and elevated outdoor space.

  • Five planned ocean-facing hospitality units above the existing hotel
  • Full-height opening glass walls planned on the ocean side to maximize beach, sunset and island views
  • Each unit planned with a king-size bed, approximately 80-inch television, high-speed Wi-Fi and Bluetooth-controlled air conditioning
  • Large-format primary baths planned with oversized two-person showers and two-person soaking tubs
  • Kitchenettes and dining areas planned in each unit
  • Guest access to the existing private pool and nearby beach
  • Existing rooftop firepit deck planned to move to the new upper level, creating a higher panoramic gathering space
