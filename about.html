Background

A developer by practice.

S. Marcus Haidet is the founder of SVF, LLC. His background combines real-estate development, hands-on construction, market research, financial modeling, capital formation and more than three decades of building businesses and assets.

Marcus grew up around architecture and construction. His father, an architect, introduced him to drafting, design and building at an early age. That foundation developed into hands-on construction experience, speculative homebuilding and residential development.

Across telecommunications, retail, automotive services, real estate and hospitality, one skill has remained consistent: identifying an underserved or underdeveloped opportunity, determining whether the economics make sense, and executing the plan required to bring it to life.

“I am a developer. My job is to take an opportunity from an idea, build every piece required to make it real, and create the structure that allows capable people to operate it.”

Capital stewardship

Experience with both personal and investor capital.

Marcus has experience developing projects with his own capital as well as private investor equity and bank financing. His approach to outside capital is built around conservative underwriting, direct sponsor involvement, clear risk disclosure and consistent communication.

The objective is not to hand a project off after capital is raised. Marcus remains directly involved in the sourcing, analysis, structure and execution of the projects he sponsors.

Today

Development, not operation.

Marcus’s role today is primarily that of a developer: identifying opportunities, researching markets, modeling projects, structuring capital, overseeing development and installing the people and systems required to operate the completed asset. His highest-value work is origination, analysis, development and expansion—not becoming the permanent operator of every function.
