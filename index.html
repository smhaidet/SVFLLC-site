The model

Development from opportunity to operation.

SVF approaches hospitality development as a complete lifecycle—from market research and acquisition through underwriting, design, construction, opening and operating-system development. The company focuses on opportunities where location, market demand and disciplined redevelopment can create durable cash flow and long-term equity growth.

Experience

Built through execution.

Marcus Haidet’s background spans real-estate development, hands-on construction, business development, capital formation and operating-company creation. That combination gives SVF a practical development perspective: understanding not only what a project should become, but what it will cost to build, how it should function and how the operating business should perform once the doors open.

Hands-on construction

Decades of direct exposure to design, building, remodeling and project execution provide a practical understanding of workmanship, cost and sequencing.

Market & financial modeling

Operator installation
