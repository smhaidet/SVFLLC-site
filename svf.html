Company

SVF, LLC

Puerto Rico hospitality development focused on acquiring, developing and repositioning well-located properties in emerging tourism markets, with selective use of private capital partnerships.

Purpose

Find the opportunity. Build the asset.

Founded by S. Marcus Haidet, SVF, LLC seeks opportunities where strong visitor demand intersects with underdeveloped, underutilized or poorly positioned real estate. The company combines market research, conservative underwriting, construction knowledge, guest-focused design and operating experience to transform those opportunities into durable hospitality assets.

Market research

Competitive occupancy, achievable room rates, tourism patterns and local supply are studied before capital is committed.

Disciplined acquisition

Long-term returns begin with the purchase. SVF focuses on a basis that leaves room for development, contingency and equity growth.

Conservative underwriting

Projects are evaluated using realistic operating assumptions, construction contingencies and downside scenarios—not optimistic projections.

Design & function

Hospitality is ultimately a customer business. Properties are designed around what travelers in the specific market actually value.

Construction oversight

Hands-on construction experience provides practical knowledge of materials, sequencing, workmanship, project costs and contractor performance.

Operating systems

Development does not end at construction. SVF installs systems, standards, trained personnel and management structures for the completed asset.

Capital relationships

Seeking aligned investment partners.

SVF is actively interested in relationships with qualified private investors, family offices, lenders and strategic capital partners for appropriate hospitality and real-estate development opportunities.

The objective is straightforward: identify compelling opportunities, structure them conservatively, protect capital through disciplined underwriting and execution, and create value through development and operation.
